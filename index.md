## About

I work at the intersection of theory and applied systems — building, breaking, and publishing on problems in machine learning, quantum error correction, and physical computing.

My recent paper, accepted at **Springer Nature CSCI 2025** (Las Vegas, Dec 3–5), focuses on identity-theft detection in critical sectors using learned models on adversarial data. Outside of papers, I prototype things that touch the real world: card readers, sensor pipelines, and graph-based learning systems built on topological data analysis.

I'm drawn to problems where the math is elegant and the implementation is messy.
